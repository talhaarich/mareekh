# Submission checklist (task 1)

**Read this first.** The guide pages I could open were the 2025 Project Submission Guide (dated 3 Oct 2025) and a Judging & Awards Guide whose text still refers to the 2024 event. A search result says the 2026 versions were released on 13 Nov 2026, but I could not open them. Everything below is therefore the 2025 rules. Open both 2026 guides at spaceappschallenge.org/resources and check anything that differs, especially the closing time and the judging criteria names.

## Project page fields (all required)
1. Project Name
2. High-Level Project Summary: what you built, how it answers the challenge, why it matters
3. Link to Project Demo: public link, no login. Either a video up to 30 seconds with English subtitles, or up to 7 slides including the title slide. Going over the limit makes the project ineligible for Global Judging.
4. Link to Final Project: public link (live site or repository), no login
5. Detailed Project Description: what it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools and languages used
6. NASA Data: which NASA data you used and how (needed for Global Awards)
7. Space Agency Partner & Other Data: every data source, resource, code, text and image used, even free or open ones
8. Use of Artificial Intelligence: which tools and how. Rules: AI images and video need a visible AI watermark; AI audio needs a note in the text and metadata; AI-written code and data need acknowledgement in the description and metadata. No NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers in AI output.
9. Two tick boxes: terms and conditions; "confirm all work is original" (all external resources must be listed in References)
10. Press "Submit for Judging". Submission is final. Images can be uploaded (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB); videos and documents must be linked.

## Eligibility traps
- The project must answer an official NASA challenge. "Create Your Own Challenge" projects are not eligible for Global Judging. **The challenge name is not in the files I was given: confirm it.**
- Every team member registered, at the same Local Event, not waitlisted. Team size 1 to 6.
- Everything in English (a non-English video needs English subtitles).
- No recognisable names, voices or likenesses of anyone under 18 in the submission.
- Teams are checked for ties to China (PRC) under NASA policy; see the Terms and Conditions.
- Deadline in the 2025 guide: closes 11:59 pm Sunday, local time of the Local Event. Our own team deadline is 20:00 Sunday (CONTEXT.md). Submission opens Saturday 14 Nov 2026, 9:00 local time.

## Judging criteria (names differ between the two 2025 guides)
Judging guide: Influence, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation. Submission guide calls the first one "Impact". Check the 2026 wording.
